Flying Wizard Support

Thanks for playing Flying Wizard! If you have a question, found a bug or need help with a purchase, email hello@apiindus.com and we'll get back to you.

Frequently asked questions

How do I steer? Drag anywhere on the screen. Hold the lightning button to boost, and double-tap for a backflip.

I bought something and it's missing. Open the Shop (or Settings) and tap Restore. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it.

How do I remove ads? Buy Remove Ads in the Shop or Settings. Optional "Second Chance" videos stay available if you want them.

How do I race a friend? Sign in to Game Center, then choose Race › 1v1 Online.

Can I turn off the music? Yes. Open Settings and switch off Music, Sound FX or Vibration.
