Flying Wizard Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This policy explains what information the Flying Wizard iOS app ("the app", "we", "us") collects and how it is used. The app is published by API Industries.

Information we collect

We do not ask you for your name, email address or any account details, and we do not run our own servers that collect personal information. Your progress, settings and purchases are stored on your device.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), IP address, general location derived from it, device model and operating system, and how you interact with ads. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track" when prompted, the advertising identifier is not shared and you will see less personalized ads. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Learn how Google uses this information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. We only receive confirmation of which items you bought so they can be unlocked in the app.

Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center to race online, Apple provides your Game Center display name to the app and shares your race position with the opponent you are matched with. This is handled by Apple's Game Center service.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Turn off tracking in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Remove most ads with the "Remove Ads" purchase.
  • Delete the app to remove all data stored on your device.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
